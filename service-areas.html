St. Augustine & Palm Coast

Up and down the ICW — sail and power, inboard and outboard. By appointment; we batch this end of the coast into dedicated run days.

Daytona Beach & New Smyrna Beach

Outboard-heavy territory: center consoles, bay boats, and PWCs. Diagnostics and full maintenance at your dock or ramp.

Titusville & Cocoa

Indian River and Mosquito Lagoon boats — skiffs, flats boats, and family bowriders. Dockside service at your marina or lift.

Merritt Island & Cape Canaveral

Our southern anchor on this coast: port-area boats, canal homes, and marina slips. Ask about Bluepoints Marina vendor-approved service.

How east coast scheduling works

Unlike our central-Florida home base, east coast runs are by appointment — we line up several boats per trip so the travel cost stays fair. Call or text with your location and what's going on with the boat, and we'll give you the next run day and an up-front travel fee. No surprises.
