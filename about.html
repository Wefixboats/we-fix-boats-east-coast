About We Fix Boats

The east coast arm of Nichols Mobile Marine

We Fix Boats is the east coast brand of Nichols Mobile Marine — a 100% mobile boat repair business based in Clermont, Florida, with 20 years in the trade.

No shop, no service writers, no "we'll call you back next week." One experienced tech, one truck, and the diagnostic tooling to find the real problem — at your dock, your marina, or your driveway.

We opened the east coast run because boat owners from St. Augustine to Merritt Island kept asking for honest mobile service: show up, diagnose it straight, fix it right, charge a fair hourly rate with parts at cost. That's the whole business model.

What you can count on

  • Straight diagnosis before any work starts
  • $150/hr labor, parts at cost — quoted up front
  • Outboards, sterndrives, inboards, and jet skis
  • The person who answers is the person who fixes it
